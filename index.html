Personal Summary

I am Dingcui Yu, a PhD student at East China Normal University, advised by Prof. Liang Shi. I am a member of the Big Data and Intelligent Systems Lab. My research interests include flash storage systems for mobile devices, such as swap systems and file system design, emerging storage interfaces including zoned UFS, and storage controller firmware design for hybrid media management.
